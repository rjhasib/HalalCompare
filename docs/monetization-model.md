# Monetization Model (Bangladesh-First)

This document outlines a Bangladesh-first monetization model for a riba-free finance comparison platform. The model prioritizes trust, transparency, and long-term relationships with institutions while keeping user interests central.

## Core Principle (Non-Negotiable)

Revenue must never influence scores, rankings, or comparisons. All monetization should be clearly labeled, optional to the user, and downstream of trust.

## Revenue Layers

### 1) Referral Traffic (Foundational)
- **What it is:** Send qualified users to Islamic bank product pages, SME finance contact pages, or agent banking locators.
- **Pricing:** CPC or per verified visit.
- **Where it appears:** Product detail pages and comparison pages (“Visit bank website”).
- **Required disclosure:** “We may receive a referral fee. This does not affect our scores or comparisons.”

### 2) SME Lead Generation (High Value)
- **What it is:** Users submit basic SME profile, financing need, and location; qualified leads are routed to banks or MFIs.
- **Pricing range:** ৳500–৳2,000 per qualified lead (Bangladesh-realistic).
- **Where it appears:** After comparison (“Check your eligibility” / “Talk to an SME advisor”).
- **Policy:** Always optional; never forced.

### 3) Agent Banking Lead Routing (Underrated)
- **What it is:** Route users searching for nearby Islamic agent banking to participating banks’ agent networks.
- **Why it works:** Agent banking is high-volume, rural-focused, and under-served digitally.
- **Who pays:** Banks with strong agent networks or Islamic windows.

### 4) Sponsored Explainers (Controlled)
- **Allowed:** Sponsored educational content (e.g., “How Murabaha SME finance works”).
- **Not allowed:** Sponsored rankings, sponsored comparison tables, or “best product” claims.
- **Disclosure:** “Sponsored educational content.”

### 5) B2B Data & Insights (Phase 2–3)
- **What it is:** Aggregated market insights, consumer demand trends, and transparency benchmarks.
- **Who buys:** Banks, NGOs, development agencies, policy researchers.
- **Not allowed:** Selling individual user data or personal financial data.

## What Must Never Be Monetized
- Ranking placement
- Score manipulation
- “Halal certification” badges or endorsements
- Fatwa endorsements
- Selling user emails

## Monetization by Phase

### Phase 1 (0–6 months)
- Referral traffic
- Organic SEO monetization
- Zero sales pressure

### Phase 2 (6–12 months)
- SME lead generation
- Agent banking routing
- First bank agreements

### Phase 3 (12–24 months)
- Sponsored explainers
- B2B dashboards
- NGO partnerships

## Revenue Realism (Conservative)
- 30k monthly visits
- 2% SME lead conversion = 600 leads
- Avg ৳1,000 per lead → ৳600,000/month
- Plus referral income → ৳200k–৳400k/month

## Trust Constitution (Publish Publicly)
- Scoring methodology
- Monetization ethics policy
- Conflict-of-interest statement

Publishing these keeps the platform regulator-safe and trust-first.
